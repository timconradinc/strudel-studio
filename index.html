❓ FAQ — parts, sections & how it works
Who made this, and how “real” is the music?

Strudel Studio is vibe-coded: the whole app was written by Claude (Anthropic’s AI) from plain-language requests. Generated music only follows general ideas about each genre — typical tempos, instruments, rhythms and chord moves — and is largely random, so treat it as a starting point, not a faithful example of the style.

What are parts?

A song is a list of parts — Intro, Verse, Chorus… — shown in the Song row. Each part holds one or more sections, and a part can come back (Verse → Chorus → Verse). Drag parts to reorder, ⧉ to copy one (the copy can be edited separately), ✕ to remove one from the song, + add me to add or repeat one.

What does each part mean?
Sections vs. parts

Sections are the actual loops of music (Cedar, Drift…). Parts group sections into the song's structure. Click a part to see and edit its sections.

What do names like Bob-M2-M1 mean?

Copies of a section share its name. When you change a copy it becomes a modified version: Bob-M1, then Bob-M2 for the next one. Change a copy of Bob-M2 and it becomes Bob-M2-M1.

Starting a song

Genre is the song's instruments: change it to swap them to that genre's — the notes, rhythms, parts and tempo stay — and the Now playing box shows it. ✚ New song (left of Play): pick a genre and a length (Standard, Simple or Complex, and how many parts), then start completely new, empty in the style of the genre, or with a random song or one-bar groove. To change one section, use the buttons beside it: 🎲 Regenerate section makes a new variation in its style, and 📥 Load section preset replaces it with a preset — both can be undone. The 🎲 on a track regenerates just that track. Classical pieces and complete examples are in 📚 Examples, next to My grooves.

How many grooves can I save?

As many as you like. Grooves are saved in this browser, so there's no fixed limit — only your browser's storage. They don't sync to other devices.

Why does the song stop at the end?

Songs play once and stop. Tick 🔁 repeat song (next to + add me in the Song row) to loop them. Single beats and 🔁 loop this section always loop.

Why does the editor jump between sections?

While the song plays, the editor always shows the section that is playing, and the Song line highlights where you are. To work on one section while hearing it, use 🔁 loop this section.

The metronome

The bar above the pads shows the beat (yellow = beat 1). The small dots in each section show the beat and how many bars of that section have played; the green line under a part shows its progress. Pick a click volume (default 25%); the music drops to half volume while the click is on, and “click only” mutes the instruments.

Can I edit the code?

Yes — edit it and press ▶ Run code. Your hand-edited code plays until you go back to the sequencer.

🎸 Chords — guitar, ukulele, bass ukulele
Shapes follow the section you're on (and the one playing). ● = finger, ◯ = open string, ✕ = don't play. On bass ukulele: ● root, ◯ fifth.
Plays: the sequencer
You're hearing your hand-edited code. Track changes are kept, but you won't hear them until you go back to the sequencer (your edits here will be replaced).
