❓ FAQ — parts, sections & how it works
What are parts?

A song is a list of parts — Intro, Verse, Chorus… — shown in the Song row. Each part holds one or more sections, and a part can come back (Verse → Chorus → Verse). Drag parts to reorder, ✕ to remove one from the song, + add me to add one.

What does each part mean?
Sections vs. parts

Sections are the actual loops of music (Cedar, Drift…). Parts group sections into the song's structure. Click a part to see and edit its sections.

What do names like Bob-M2-M1 mean?

Copies of a section share its name. When you change a copy it becomes a modified version: Bob-M1, then Bob-M2 for the next one. Change a copy of Bob-M2 and it becomes Bob-M2-M1.

Randomize, Start a new groove, New song?

🎲 Randomize current rolls a new groove for the section you're editing only; the rest of the song stays. ✨ Start a new groove builds a whole new song in the current style. At the top, 🥁 New beat (the default) makes a new one-section groove in a new genre and switches the Preset / style menu to it; ▾ lists 🆕 New song (random genre, full song) and the other song actions. 🆕 New empty song starts from nothing.

How many grooves can I save?

As many as you like. Grooves are saved in this browser, so there's no fixed limit — only your browser's storage. They don't sync to other devices.

Why does the song stop at the end?

Songs play once and stop. Tick 🔁 repeat song (next to + add me in the Song row) to loop them. Single beats and 🔁 loop this section always loop.

Why does the editor jump between sections?

While the song plays, the editor always shows the section that is playing, and the Song line highlights where you are. To work on one section while hearing it, use 🔁 loop this section.

The metronome

The bar above the pads shows the beat (yellow = beat 1). The small dots in each section show the beat and how many bars of that section have played; the green line under a part shows its progress. Pick a click volume (default 25%); the music drops to half volume while the click is on, and “click only” mutes the instruments.

Can I edit the code?

Yes — edit it and press ▶ Run code. Your hand-edited code plays until you go back to the sequencer.

Shapes follow the section you're on (and the one playing). ● = finger, ◯ = open string, ✕ = don't play. On bass ukulele: ● root, ◯ fifth.
